# Contributing to Nurse Voices Matter

Thank you for caring about this work. There are many ways to help.

## Report a Bug

Found something broken? Email **sharonda@nursebloc.io** with:
- What you were doing when it broke
- What you expected to happen
- What happened instead
- Your device/browser if relevant

## Suggest a Feature

Have an idea to make the tool better? Email **sharonda@nursebloc.io** with:
- What the feature does
- Why it matters for bedside nurses
- How you'd use it

**Examples:**
- "Add Spanish version"
- "Add hospital/system name field"
- "Print flyer in different sizes"

## Share Your Story

Used Nurse Voices Matter? Tell us about it:
- How many comments you submitted
- Which specialties joined in
- Tips that helped other nurses
- Anything you'd change

Email **sharonda@nursebloc.io** — we read every message.

## Fork & Adapt

This tool is MIT licensed. You can:
- **Translate it** to another language
- **Modify it** for your organization
- **Host it** on your own server
- **Print it** as a worksheet

Just keep the original attribution and license.

**Questions?** Email sharonda@nursebloc.io
