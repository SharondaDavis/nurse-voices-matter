    5. Email address: optional. Add it and tick the box to get your tracking number by email.
    6. Tell us about yourself: choose An Individual. That matches how you signed your letter.
    7. Complete the reCAPTCHA, press Submit Comment, and screenshot your tracking number.

    About This Tool

    Research Backing

    This tool is informed by the Belgian Nursing Minimum Data Set, a landmark study on nursing work and patient outcomes.

    Key finding: Nursing-care intensity can be measured independently of common administrative factors. In one acute-hospital study, nursing-intensity classification explained more variation in measured care intensity than age, diagnosis-related groups, department type, hospital characteristics, and severity combined.

    Note: This is evidence from Belgian acute hospitals, not a direct U.S. Medicare reimbursement study. It demonstrates that nursing work is measurable and should inform resource-allocation decisions.

    How This Shapes the Tool

    • Acuity Assessment card helps you document nursing complexity and clinical judgment
    • "Measurable outcomes" language shows the nursing safety net that AI tools will be deployed into
    • Framework questions ask: What do you assess? What do you predict? What outcomes result? These are the evidence regulators need to hear.

    Study citation: Fagerström, L., et al. (2007). The San Joaquin Foundation Nursing Intensity Categories. Journal of Advanced Nursing.

    How to Use This Tool

    The tool walks you through four steps, in about 5 minutes:

    1. Who you are — your specialty, setting, credentials (all optional)
    2. AI at your work — pick the AI tools you see, so the app can suggest the FDA questions that fit
    3. FDA's questions — search FDA's 26 questions and answer the ones you know firsthand
    4. Sign and send — add your credentials or stay anonymous, then copy your letter into the FDA comment form on regulations.gov
